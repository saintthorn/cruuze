Cruuze logos: modified delta

Three takes on ∆. For reference, the plain solid triangle they have to stay clear of:

A solid white triangle on black is already a well-known developer brand.

1. Open delta

A delta that has not closed yet: a change in progress, with the last stroke in the accent. Outlined and broken, so it reads as the letter ∆ and not as a solid triangle.

Engineering · Cruuze×

2. Sail

A delta with wind in it, on a waterline. It is the only idea so far that says "cruise" outright, and its curved edge keeps it away from the straight-sided triangles already in use.

Engineering · Cruuze×

3. Rows

A delta stacked from the rows of an issue list, the top one in the accent. Reads as a list and as priority, and no outline to confuse with another triangle.
