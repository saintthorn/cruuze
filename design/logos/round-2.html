Cruuze logos, round 2

Each idea as a lockup on dark and light, then the mark in one colour, in the hacker theme, as an app icon at 64, 32 and 16 px, and in a browser tab.

1. Lanes

The double u, nested: one u inside the other, like lanes on a track. It is the name’s own oddity, it cannot be mistaken for a w, and two arcs survive at 16 px. Motion: a dot running the lane.

2. Slots

A solid tile with the double u cut out of it. The cut-outs are also two board columns, with one card landed. Solid shapes hold up better than strokes when small, and nobody in the category owns this silhouette.

Engineering · Cruuze×

3. Hold

Get up to speed, then hold it: an acceleration curve that flattens into a steady line. It says cruise control and forecast in one stroke. Motion: the line draws on and the flat part pulses.

Engineering · Cruuze×

4. Tilde

The home directory of the terminal look (~/cruuze) and a wave you cruise on. One glyph, already on every keyboard, so it carries the hacker heritage into a cleaner brand.
